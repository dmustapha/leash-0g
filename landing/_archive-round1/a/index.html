How it works

Four moves. The agent never holds the keys to its own limits.

  1. 1

    Bind

    Point your agent's wallet at a LEASH account. Set a per-transfer cap, a spending window, an allowlist, and an expiry.

  2. 2

    Watch

    See it reason live. Step in before it acts, not after.

  3. 3

    Contain

    The contract refuses anything outside the leash, no matter what the agent was tricked into asking for.

  4. 4

    Prove

    Every decision hash-chained, encrypted to you alone, anchored on 0G.
