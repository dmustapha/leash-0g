03 · Contain

Then it pulls.
The leash holds.

The contract refuses anything outside the leash, no matter what the agent was tricked into asking for. Four limits, enforced by a contract on 0G Chain:

  • Per-transfer cap. No single send above your number.
  • Spending window. A budget per period, not a blank check.
  • Allowlist. Only addresses you approved, ever.
  • Expiry. The leash outlives your attention, not your intent.

One move
ends everything.

Revoke is a single transaction. After it, the contract refuses every act, even ones already queued. We ran it live: it fail-closed.
