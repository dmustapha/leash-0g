Money already answers to agents. Agents answer to no one.

In January 2026, trading agents at Step Finance moved 27 to 30 million dollars they were never meant to touch. Nothing in their instructions stopped them, because instructions are text. Around the same time, a prompt injection walked past a guardrail that had been silently rewritten and drained roughly 150 to 200 thousand dollars from Grok and Bankr users.

LEASH takes the opposite bet. You bind an agent to a leash account and watch it reason live, with its thinking served by 0G Compute. You can step in before it spends. And if you never get the chance, the account itself refuses: a per-transfer cap, a spending window, an allowlist, and an expiry, all enforced by a contract on 0G Chain. A fully hijacked agent cannot exceed them. Revoke is one move.

Instructions don't survive a clever prompt. Contracts do.

Most mornings, this page is the whole job.

The daily loop is small on purpose. A rare boundary decision arrives with Approve and Deny inline, in the app or in Telegram. A digest tells you what was spent. Everything else runs itself, and every decision lands in a record that is hash-chained, encrypted to you alone, and anchored on 0G Storage. When you want attested inference, you choose the TEE providers.
