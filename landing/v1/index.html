What the contract holds, even against a hijacked agent

Per-transfer cap
No single spend can exceed the amount you set. The contract refuses, not the agent.
Spending window
A rolling budget. When the window is spent, the account is quiet until it resets.
Allowlist
Money only moves to addresses you approved. Everything else bounces.
Expiry
The leash has an end date. After it, the agent holds nothing.
Revoke
One move closes the account to the agent. Mid-thought, mid-transaction, any time.

Limits enforced by a contract on 0G Chain. Attested inference where you choose TEE providers.
