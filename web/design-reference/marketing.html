How the leash holds

Three bounds it cannot loosen. One cut you can make.

The policy lives in the agent's ENS record. The payment rail reads it before every settle. The agent signs — it never sets.

01CAP

A ceiling it can't raise

Per-transfer, daily and weekly caps are fields in the ENS policy record. The rail reads them, compares, and settles only within them.

MAX_PER_TRANSFER = $500 · MAX_DAILY = $1,000
02ALLOWLIST

A reach it can't widen

Funds only move to payees the owner added. The agent has no path to extend its own allowlist.

03REVOKE

One write that cuts it off

Revoke is a single on-chain write. Every rail resolving that record stops honoring the agent — at once.

HONEST BOUNDARY

What the leash actually is

Control, not custody. Caps are a soft budget the rail enforces on settle; the allowlist defaults so the agent can't widen its own reach; revoke is on-chain-resolved. It is not a trustless vault — it's a leash you hold and can cut.
