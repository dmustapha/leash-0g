Signing-intent policy

Grant your agent the right to sign off-chain permit/intent messages for a reviewed protocol — bounded by the same per-token budget as any on-chain move (signing ≤ calling). Every signable protocol is a reviewed descriptor; a typed-data with no descriptor is refused, never guessed. The grant is signed on-chain with the engine-computed domainSeparator + typeHash, so what you approve and what the wall enforces are identical.

N-01/N-02 run at review time: the domain must bind chainId + verifyingContract, and every field must be a static 32-byte word. A mis-authored descriptor is a loud error here — never a silent on-chain “never-validates”.
